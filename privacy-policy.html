Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Welcome to Creative Play ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Our Pinterest Automation App and website operate primarily as content delivery and curation platforms. We do not require you to create an account on our website, and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number directly through this website.

2. Pinterest API and Data Usage

Our associated Pinterest application uses the Pinterest API to function. When our application connects to your Pinterest account (if applicable), it operates with the permissions you explicitly grant during the OAuth authorization process.

What we access: We may access your boards and pins to publish content.
What we DO NOT do: We do not store your Pinterest data, personal profile information, or access tokens on our servers. All tokens and credentials remain locally on your machine where the automation script is run.

While our content is centered around kids' learning toys, our website and services are intended for use by adults (parents, educators, and guardians). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe we may have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
